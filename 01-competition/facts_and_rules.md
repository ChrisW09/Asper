## Format and rules (validated)

### Round 1 — Prepared case (25 % of score)
- Released ~2 weeks ahead by e-mail *(2025: released Feb 5, due Feb 19, 10:00 CT — registration day)*
- Submission: **.pptx by e-mail** to aspercasecomps@umanitoba.ca, file named "[Team Name] Prepared Case"; **late submissions are placed at the bottom of the pool**
- 25-min presentation + 15-min Q&A; yellow card at 5 min, red at 1 min (both periods)
- Unlimited slides/exhibits; every member must speak
- All resources allowed, **including coach/advisor input**; contacting case-sponsor employees prohibited
- **No third-party slide templates** for the prepared case
- 2026 client: Jazz Winnipeg ("Jazz Winnipeg at a Crossroads") — pitch a radical new operational idea

### Round 2 — Boardroom case (30 % of score)
- On-site prep: 4 h *(2025, 2026)*; staggered slots; deck + **one-page black-and-white executive brief** saved to a provided USB stick at a fixed pickup time
- **Protected 3 minutes:** uninterrupted recommendation using **one summary slide only — appendices blocked**; red card at 1 min
- Then **22-minute discussion** with judges, appendices accessible; yellow/red cards at 5/1 min
- Seated boardroom style, wireless keyboard/mouse provided; judges get the one-page brief in advance
- Every member must speak during the discussion (not necessarily in the protected 3 minutes)
- No coach/outside contact after release; one laptop per student; prebuilt own templates permitted; no phones/smartwatches
- 2026 client: Coal & Canary — elevate brand from premium to luxury (4 judges + company representative)

### Round 3 — Traditional case → **10-hour case in 2027** (45 % of score)
- **2027:** the schedule lists a "10-Hour Case": preparation Fri Feb 19 (printed "Feb 22"), presentations and finals Sat Feb 20. The rules text still says "Traditional Case" and gives no prep length — treat the two as the same round until confirmed (open question 1)
- *(2025, 2026)* On-site prep: 3 h; staggered slots; deck to provided USB, named "[Team Name] Traditional Case"
- 20-min presentation + 10-min Q&A; yellow/red at 5/1 min (both periods)
- Unlimited slides/exhibits; every member must speak
- No coach/outside contact after release; one laptop per student; prebuilt templates permitted; no phones/smartwatches
- 2026 client: Access Credit Union — service innovations to engage young people
- **Unknown for the 10-hour format** (all in open question 1): whether prep is staggered, whether teams may leave the room or get meals, whether the deck is handed in at the end of prep or on presentation morning, and whether internet/GenAI and the no-contact rule apply unchanged across the whole day (assume yes)

### Final round
- Top 3 by weighted score (25/30/45); 2027 schedule: "10-Hour Case Finals Presentation" on Sat Feb 20 — finalists re-present their third-round deck; tie → higher traditional-case score advances *(2025 guide)*; the 2026/27-cycle page text expands this into a cascade — performance in the highest-weighted rounds first, then rubric-category performance by weight. Assume the cascade applies in 2027 (`handbook.md` §6)
- New judge panel; finalists escorted to a holding room; **decks frozen** (no changes)
- 20-min presentation + 15-min Q&A; yellow/red at 5/1
- Advisors may not communicate with finalist teams until after their presentation; winners announced at the gala

### General rules
- Competition conducted **entirely in English**
- **PowerPoint (.pptx) only** — other software (e.g., Prezi) not permitted
- **Internet allowed during prep** — incl. login-required university databases, file sharing, and **Generative AI**
- Prebuilt own slide templates allowed (built outside prep time)
- **School anonymity: identifying your school of origin to a judge at any point = disqualification**; coach communication during presentation/Q&A = disqualification
- Cases are confidential: no reproduction/sharing; no discussing outside the competition context
- Slide clickers provided; volunteers monitor compliance; late arrival = no additional prep or presentation time; slide decks are shared with case sponsors after the event
- Dress code: **business professional** for every competition context

## Official judging rubric (2025 Event Guide — applied to all rounds)

| Category | Weight | Judges ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Implementation & resources | **2.5×** | Clear path to follow, with resources, time, and financial considerations? |
| Analysis | 2.0× | Assessment/recommendation substantiated by research, logic, quant + qual analysis? |
| Recommendation | 2.0× | Effective, clear, comprehensive, strategic fit? |
| Communicating effectively | 1.0× | Clear, professional, compelling presentation and readable deck? |
| Framing the problem | 1.0× | Issues wrapped into a clear, solvable summary (primary + secondary)? |
| Impact | 1.0× | Quantitative and qualitative benefit to the organization? |
| Measurement, risks & mitigation | 1.0× | Progress measurement and realistic risk mitigation? |

**Training implication:** Implementation & Resources is the single heaviest category — every mock presentation must end with a concrete, resourced, costed implementation plan and KPIs.
