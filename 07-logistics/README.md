# 07 · Logistics — getting there and operating there

Bielefeld to Winnipeg and back, plus the script for the five days on the ground.

**The files, in the order they become urgent**

1. [`registration_email_draft.md`](registration_email_draft.md) — **W1, send this first.** Ready to go to
   `aspercasecomps@umanitoba.ca`; it registers the team and puts all seven open questions (eligibility,
   fees, agenda, roster requirements, hotel block, rule deltas, invitation letter) into one exchange.
2. [`travel_logistics.md`](travel_logistics.md) — the admin track that runs from August to February: eTA for
   German citizens, flight routings and price bands, the team hotel, airport transfer, campus transfers, the
   **−30 °C clothing list**, money and SIM, insurance (**statutory German GKV does not cover Canada**), the
   7-hour jet-lag plan, and the booking countdown.
3. [`competition_day_runbook.md`](competition_day_runbook.md) — hour-by-hour sheets for all five days, the
   standing rules that apply all week, and the contingency plans. This is the file the team operates from in
   Winnipeg; print it.

**The dates, and the one caveat.** The competition is **February 18–22, 2027**, which falls
**Thursday–Monday**. Both previous editions ran **Wednesday–Sunday** (2025: Wed Feb 19 – Sun Feb 23; 2026:
Wed Feb 18 – Sun Feb 22), so 2027 would be the first Thursday start. The day-sheets assume the published
Thu–Mon window with the round order inherited from 2025, and say so in place. The travel plan is
deliberately robust to both readings: arriving **Feb 16/17** and returning **Feb 22/23** covers a Wednesday
start as well as the published Thursday one — **but do not book non-refundable tickets outside that envelope
until the 2027 agenda is confirmed.**
