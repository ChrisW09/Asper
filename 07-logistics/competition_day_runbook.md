# Competition-Day Runbook — ABCC 2027, February 15–20

Hour-by-hour operating sheets for the six days. **Dates and the order of rounds follow the official 2027 schedule** (published autumn 2026: Mon arrival · Tue opening + prepared · Wed boardroom · Thu excursion · Fri 10-hour prep · Sat 10-hour round, finals, awards dinner — `01-competition/facts_and_rules.md`, "The 2027 schedule"). **Clock times are still the 2025 Event Guide pattern** — the 2027 page gives dates only — so treat every time as *expected* until the organizers' agenda arrives; the 10-hour day has no precedent and its times are our planning assumption. All times Winnipeg local (CST = UTC−6).

## Standing rules (all days)

Business professional in every competition context; boots for outside, shoes carried. Never name HSBI in front of judges — team letter and fictional consulting name only. Phones and smartwatches surrendered before every prep window. The strategist owns the clock; the coach owns nothing after a timed case releases. One 15-minute debrief per round, then the topic is closed. Hydrate; eat before, not during, the 3- and 4-hour prep windows — the 10-hour day is the exception, with meals scheduled into the clock. Every evening: chargers plugged, template file opened and tested, next day's call time confirmed in the team chat.

## Contingencies

Late transfer or lost in the building → leaving 30 min earlier than needed is the plan, not the exception. Laptop failure in prep → the team continues on three; every file lives on the shared drive and a USB stick from minute one. Team member ill → roles collapse per the cross-cover matrix in `03-team/role_playbooks.md`; all four must still speak if physically present. Deck won't open from USB → the .pptx was saved twice (USB + laptop); calmly ask volunteers — the organizers accept no responsibility, so redundancy is ours.
