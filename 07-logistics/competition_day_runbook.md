# Competition-Day Runbook — ABCC 2027, February 18–22

Hour-by-hour operating sheets for the five days. Times follow the **2025 Event Guide pattern** (the only published full schedule), mapped onto the published 2027 window of Feb 18–22, which falls **Thursday–Monday**. Treat every weekday below as *expected*: both prior editions began on a **Wednesday**, so the real 2027 week may instead run Wed Feb 17 – Sun Feb 21 (`01-competition/facts_and_rules.md`, "The 2027 weekday question"). The *shape* of each day — registration and draw, then prepared, boardroom, traditional with finals — is what this runbook drills; rewrite the dates the day the 2027 guide arrives. All times Winnipeg local (CST = UTC−6, 7 hours behind Bielefeld).

## Standing rules (all days)

Business professional in every competition context; boots for outside, shoes carried. Never name HSBI in front of judges — team letter and fictional consulting name only. Phones and smartwatches surrendered before every prep window. The strategist owns the clock; the coach owns nothing after a timed case releases. One 15-minute debrief per round, then the topic is closed. Hydrate; eat before, not during, prep windows where possible. Every evening: chargers plugged, template file opened and tested, next day's call time confirmed in the team chat.

## Contingencies

Late shuttle or lost in the building → taxi numbers in `07-logistics/travel_logistics.md`; leaving 30 min earlier than needed is the plan, not the exception. Laptop failure in prep → the team continues on three; every file lives on the shared drive and a USB stick from minute one. Team member ill → roles collapse per the cross-cover matrix in `03-team/role_playbooks.md`; all four must still speak if physically present. Deck won't open from USB → the .pptx was saved twice (USB + laptop); calmly ask volunteers — the organizers accept no responsibility, so redundancy is ours.
