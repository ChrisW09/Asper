**Subject:** International team interest — ABCC 2027 (HSBI, Germany)

Dear Ms. Jayasuriya, dear Mr. Harrison,

I am a professor at Hochschule Bielefeld (HSBI) — University of Applied Sciences and Arts in Bielefeld, Germany, and I would like to enter a team in the **Asper Business Case Competition 2027** (February 15–20). We have followed the competition's development with great interest — not least Babeș-Bolyai's participation and win in 2026 — and would be delighted to bring a second international team to Winnipeg.

Our team would consist of **four Masters-level students** with backgrounds in finance, innovation management, computer science, and general business administration, accompanied by myself as faculty advisor. Before we finalize planning, could you help us with the following:

1. **Eligibility and entry:** Is a team of Masters-level students from a German business school eligible for the 2027 edition, and what does the registration process for an international school look like? Is there a participation fee, and what does it cover?
2. **The 10-hour case and the schedule:** We have seen the 2027 schedule on your website — thank you. Could you confirm that the **10-hour case** is the third round in place of the three-hour traditional case, with the same 20-minute presentation, 10-minute Q&A, 45 % weighting, and finals? And is the "February 22 — 10-Hour Case Preparation" line meant to read **February 19**? Anything you can share about the 10-hour day itself (start time and staggering, meals, whether teams may leave the room, when the deck is handed in) would help us prepare. Finally, when do you expect the prepared case to be released and due, and at what time are registration and the division draw on February 15?
3. **Team formalities:** What information do you need from us and by when (team roster, bios, photos, fictional team name, waivers)? Is there a separate registration step for me as faculty advisor?
4. **2027 rules package:** Do you expect any changes against the 2025 Event Guide baseline we are training on — in particular around the use of generative AI during timed prep (permitted in 2025), any disclosure duties, or case-confidentiality terms that would affect which tools we may use?

Please let me know if a short call would be easier — I am generally available afternoons CET.

Thank you very much, and congratulations on building such a distinctive competition — the three-round format is precisely why we want to take part.

Kind regards,

Prof. Dr. Christoph Weisser
Hochschule Bielefeld (HSBI), Germany
[phone] · [institutional e-mail @hsbi.de]
