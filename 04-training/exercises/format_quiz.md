# Format Mastery Quiz — Know the Competition Cold

Every team member must score **25/25** by the end of Week 3 (retake weekly until then). The format decides tactics; nobody walks into Winnipeg unsure of a rule. Source for every answer: `01-competition/facts_and_rules.md` and `01-competition/handbook.md`. Coach quizzes verbally, shuffled order, ~15 minutes.

## Questions

**Rounds and timing**
1. Name the three scored rounds and their weights.
2. Presentation + Q&A length in the prepared round?
3. Prep time and format of the boardroom round?
4. What may be shown during the protected 3 minutes — and what is blocked?
5. Presentation + Q&A length in the third (traditional) round — and how long is its prep in 2026 versus 2027?
6. When do the yellow and red cards come, and in which segments?
7. Who advances to the final, and what changes there (three things)?
8. How are ties for a finals place broken?

**Deliverables and mechanics**
9. In what file format must every deck be built, and what is explicitly not permitted?
10. How and by when is the prepared-case deck submitted (2025 baseline), and what happens if it is late?
11. How are the boardroom and traditional decks handed in?
12. What extra artifact does the boardroom round require, and who reads it when?
13. What are the exact file-naming conventions?
14. May we build slide templates before the competition? Any restriction?

**Rules and conduct**
15. What may we access on our laptops during timed prep? Name three notable inclusions.
16. What two devices are banned during prep and presentations?
17. From when until when is coach contact forbidden, and what does a violation cost?
18. What is the school-anonymity rule and its penalty?
19. Who must speak, in which rounds, with what boardroom nuance?
20. What happens if a team arrives late to its slot?
21. May we contact employees of a case sponsor? May we discuss the case outside the competition?

**Judging and context**
22. Name the seven rubric categories with their multipliers, heaviest first.
23. Which category weighs most, and what does that mean for how we build decks?
24. Where and when is ABCC 2027 — and which round falls on which day?
25. Who won ABCC 2026, and why does that matter to us?
