## Ground rules for every exercise

Timed, out loud, in English. Solutions stay closed until the attempt is finished. Results that feed the rubric (simulations, graded drills) go into `02-plan/simulation_log.md`; everything else is logged by a one-line note in the team chat ("W7 midweek: 3.1 done, titles improving, Q&A stems next"). If a week collapses, the priority order is: simulation > mini-case > workbook item > individual drills — protect the simulations above all.
