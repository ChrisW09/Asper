# Simulation Log — ABCC 2027

Running record of every scored simulation from `02-plan/week_by_week_plan.md`, and the single source of truth for the W20 half-term review and the W26 taper decisions.

## Instructions

1. The acting judge (coach or external) scores every simulation live on `01-competition/judging_scoresheet.md` — seven categories, 1–10 each, weighted total out of 105 (Implementation & Resources ×2.5, Analysis ×2.0, Recommendation ×2.0, all others ×1.0).
2. The general-business lead transfers the scores here **within 24 h**, together with the actual date and actual prep time (record deviations from plan).
3. Each person leaves the debrief with **one improvement goal** (≤ 10 words), recorded in role order **Fin / Inn / CS / GB**, separated by " / ".
4. Compare like with like: judge trends within a format (Traditional #1 → #2 at 3 h, 10-hour #1 → #2, Boardroom #1 → #2 → #3), not across formats. The 3-h and 10-h third-round sims share the 20 + 10 delivery but not the depth judges should expect — score the 10-hour runs against a higher bar on Analysis and I&R.
6. *Re-planned 2026-10-31* for the published 2027 schedule (10-hour third round, competition Feb 15–20): sim 6 became a 10-hour run and the W25 pair follows the 2027 order.
5. Internal targets (ours, not official): honest baseline at W8 with no target; a rising trend per format; by W25 **≥ 85/105 with no category below 6**.

Column key: **I&R** Implementation & Resources · **Ana** Analysis · **Rec** Recommendation · **Com** Communicating Effectively · **Frm** Framing the Problem · **Imp** Impact · **MRM** Measurement, Risks & Mitigation.

## Goal carried forward rule

A personal goal is **cleared** only when the next simulation shows visible progress — the related category score rises or the judge explicitly confirms it. Otherwise it **carries forward**, marked "(c/f)" in the goals column, until cleared. A goal carried twice triggers a dedicated 30-minute drill block in the next midweek session, owned by the coach. Nobody holds more than two open goals — if a new one arises, park the less score-relevant one and note it at the W20/W26 review.
