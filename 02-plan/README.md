# 02 · Plan — when we do what

The training programme: its logic, its calendar, and the record of how it is going.

**The three files, and which one wins**

1. [`week_by_week_plan.md`](week_by_week_plan.md) — **the operational document and the authority on dates.**
   Dated weeks from W1 (Aug 3, 2026) to the debrief, each with the session, materials, role homework, and
   admin milestone. Open it every week. Where any document in this repository disagrees about *when*, this
   file governs.
2. [`preparation_plan.md`](preparation_plan.md) — the **concept** behind the calendar: design principles,
   the four roles, Phases 0–5, the prepared-case sprint, the risk register. Read once, revisit when
   deciding whether a change to the calendar is sound. Its phase numbers are concept phases and
   deliberately differ from the calendar's. The season briefing that walks the team through the
   programme is presented from [`preparation_plan.pptx`](preparation_plan.pptx) (26 slides, built from
   `tools/slides/`).
3. [`simulation_log.md`](simulation_log.md) — the results record for all ten planned simulations. Filled in
   **within 24 h** of every simulation by the general-business lead; drives the W20 half-term review and
   the W26 taper decisions.

**Current position:** W1, Aug 3–9, 2026 — kick-off week. 29 weeks to the competition.
