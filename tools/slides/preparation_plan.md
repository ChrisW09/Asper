## Four design principles

- **Train for the weights.** Boardroom 30 % + round 3 45 % = 75 % of the
  score — most training time goes into timed on-site simulations
- **Leads, not silos.** Each student owns a lens; everyone carries the
  whole storyline
- **Competition-identical conditions.** Every mock under the real rules
- **Feedback beats volume.** Every simulation ends with a structured debrief

Within every case: the rubric weights **Implementation & Resources 2.5×**,
Analysis 2.0×, Recommendation 2.0×.

# The weekly rhythm

## Three fixed slots (from October)

- **1 × 3–5 h weekend block** — simulation or workshop
- **1 × 60–90 min midweek session** — debrief + skills
- **30 min individual drills** — business math · vocabulary · slide re-work

Time commitment: **4–6 h/week rising to 8–10 h** near the competition.
Lectures run through Phase 3 — hence the weekend blocks.

## Four leads, one storyline

| Student | Lead role | Owns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finance | **Team CFO** | Model, valuation, sensitivity, ask |
| Innovation | **Creative lead** | Ideas, differentiation, value prop |
| Computer Science | **Analytics & tech lead** | Exhibits, AI feasibility, modelling |
| General Business | **Strategist & integrator** | Framing, storyline, the clock |

Rotate the **opening speaker** and the **Q&A quarterback** across mock
cases — all four credible in every position.

# Phase by phase

## Foundations — W1–W5

**Goal: shared language and method.** Semester break — flexible scheduling.

- W1 kick-off: rules, charter, cadence · **registration e-mail** out
- Case method 101 — read a case in 30 min, issue trees, MECE
- Framework toolkit workshop — train the **five-minute selector**
- Watch + dissect case-final videos with the official rubric
- Milestone (W5): **mini-crack** — Manitoba Hydro brief, 60 min,
  one-page recommendation

## Skill building — W6–W10

**Goal: from analysis to persuasive presentation.**

- Storylining, action titles, the reusable skeleton
- **Team .pptx template** started — 16:9, anonymity-proof
- W8: first full crack, generous 6 h — Manitoba Museum 2025, 25+15 to coach
- **Q&A bootcamp + first gauntlet** — answer-first, bridging, quarterback;
  never "we didn't look at that"
- 3-min pitch formula + boardroom discussion technique

## Simulations — W11–W20

**Goal: speed, consistency, resilience.** Alternating weekend simulations,
debrief weeks in between.

- *Round 3* most often — the **45 %-weight round**: 3-h speed runs, and
  **10-hour** days in W17 and W25 for the 2027 format
- *Boardroom:* one slide + one-page brief + 3-min pitch + **22-min discussion**
- External judges for at least 4 sessions — unfamiliar faces change behaviour
- W20 half-term review: score trends, role rotation, gap list for January

Milestone: two consecutive sims trending up · everyone has opened at
least once — targets live in `02-plan/simulation_log.md`.

## Christmas dip · January peak — W21–W26

**W21–W22 (light load):** individual only — math drills twice, English
case vocabulary, one final video with rubric.

**W23–W26 (peak):**

- W23–24: **Klangfest Bielefeld** — prepared-round dress rehearsal,
  14-day mini-sprint + 25+15 before external judges
- W25: **competition-week simulation in the 2027 order** — boardroom
  Friday → 10-hour case Saturday → present Sunday + finals re-run
- W26: targeted repairs, gauntlet #3 — **taper begins, no new content**

## Same rules, every time

Every simulation is competition-identical:

- Staggered release · timed prep (3, 4 or 10 h) · **one laptop per student**
- No phones, no smartwatches · internet + GenAI allowed
- **No coach contact after release**
- .pptx to "USB" at pickup time · all four speak · yellow/red cards

Plus deliberate **failure modes**: unfamiliar industry, heavy data exhibits,
an ambiguous case, a "failed" projector.

## Score it, debrief it, improve one thing

- Every simulation scored with the **official ABCC rubric** — 7 categories,
  Implementation & Resources 2.5× on top
- Scores tracked over time in `02-plan/simulation_log.md`
- Video recording · judge-style scoring · **one improvement goal per
  person** for the next round
- After each mock: 15 minutes of deliberately hostile Q&A

## The 13-day prepared-case sprint

**Goal: a submission-ready 25-minute deck, on time.**

- **Days 1–2:** deep read, client research — public sources only
- **Days 3–5:** parallel workstreams by lead · daily 30-min sync
- **Days 6–7:** option choice, title-only storyline, **coach review #1** —
  allowed in this round, use it
- **Days 8–10:** deck build, model hardened, appendix bank
- **Days 11–12:** two dress rehearsals 25+15, coach review #2
- **Day 13 (Sat Feb 13):** freeze, then **submit before travel** — late =
  bottom of the pool

## Final polish — protect energy

- One light boardroom + one 3-h round-3 drill mid-sprint —
  **no new content marathon**
- Prepared-case run-throughs to internalize, not memorize — Q&A practice
  beats delivery now
- **Kit check:** adapters, chargers, dongles, clicker, backup USBs,
  offline copies of everything, business attire
- Competition-day charter: sleep, meals, the fixed prep-window protocol —
  first 20 min silent reading, last 30 min rehearsal, always; the
  10-hour clock for Friday; Thursday's excursion is for recovery

# When a week collapses

## Protect the cadence above all

The calendar's own rule: *"Adjust freely; protect the simulation cadence
above all."*

1. **The weekend simulation** — timed reps are the training; never skip,
   only move
2. **The debrief** — feedback beats volume; an unscored sim is half a sim
3. **The 30-min individual drills** — flex first, but they compound

Everything else — clinics, videos, homework — reschedules around these.
