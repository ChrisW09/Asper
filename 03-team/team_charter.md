# Team Charter — HSBI @ ABCC 2027

To be discussed, amended, and signed by all five of us at kick-off (W1). It exists so that in week 19, when everyone is tired, the rules are already agreed.

## What we are here for

We travel to Winnipeg to compete seriously — Babeș-Bolyai proved in 2026 that a first-time international team can win the ABCC outright — and to come back better presenters, faster analysts, and a tighter team than any seminar could make us. Both goals rank above individual credit.

## Commitments

Each of us commits to the training cadence in `02-plan/week_by_week_plan.md` (one weekend block + one midweek session + 30 minutes of drills, rising toward February), to preparing for sessions rather than attending them, and to flagging conflicts (exams, work, life) **two weeks ahead** so sessions move rather than die. The coach commits to organized sessions, honest feedback, external judges for the graded simulations, and handling all organizer communication.

## How we work

Roles are leads, not silos (`03-team/role_playbooks.md`); everyone can cover every part, and the opener rotates through all four of us in training. Feedback follows the scoresheet (`01-competition/judging_scoresheet.md`): two strengths, two gaps, one drill — about the work, never the person, and everyone takes their turn receiving it. Disagreements on case content are settled by argument and evidence with a hard time-box; if the box expires, the strategist decides the storyline, the finance lead decides the numbers, and we commit to the chosen line as one team — in front of judges, nobody relitigates.

## Non-negotiables

Simulation conditions are real conditions: real clocks, real device rules, no coach contact after release, .pptx to the deadline — every time, so Winnipeg feels like a Tuesday. The anonymity rule is absolute from the first mock: no HSBI names, logos, or hints in any deck, document, or anecdote used in front of judges. Deadlines bind: the person who owns a deliverable owns telling the team **early** if it is at risk. And the trip is also a trip: we attend the social program fully, represent our school well (without naming it to judges), and nobody's competition result changes how we treat each other at breakfast.

## If it stops working

Anyone can call a charter talk at any time — fifteen minutes, coach moderates, problem named plainly, adjustment agreed. Persistent no-shows or unpreparedness first cost the person the most attractive speaking slot, then — coach's call after a direct conversation — the competition seat, because four prepared teammates outrank five names on a roster.
