# Practice Case Sources

Simulation material for Phases 2–4, in order of value. Aim for variety across the three round formats and the recurring ABCC client types.

## 1. Real past ABCC cases (best material — use first)

**Eleven official past cases (2023–2026) exist across ten clients** — 2023 ran both rounds on Manitoba Hydro. Six PDFs are mirrored in this repo under `01-competition/past-case-pdfs/`; the other five (2024 cases 1 and 3, all three 2026 cases) must be downloaded from the URLs in `01-competition/facts_and_rules.md`. All eleven are profiled with **re-crack briefs** (task + guiding questions + what a winning answer needed) in `01-competition/past_cases.md`. Recommended order: Manitoba Museum 2025 (prepared) → People Corporation 2025 (traditional) → AROW Global 2025 (boardroom) → Independent Jewellers 2024 (boardroom) → Access CU / Jazz Winnipeg / Coal & Canary 2026 from the re-crack briefs → Manitoba Hydro 2023 pair for framing drills.

## 3. Other sources

- **Other competitions' released cases and finals videos** (e.g., John Molson MBA ICC, JMUCC) — winning-team videos are prime study material for delivery and Q&A.
- **Ivey / Harvard case collections** — short cases (5–15 pages) via the university library for timed structuring drills.
- **Consulting-club casebooks** (free online) — market sizing and structuring drills.
- **Self-written mini-cases** — one-page prompts on real OWL companies with 3–4 public exhibits; cheap and fresh.

## The 2027 pattern to train against

The client formula has been stable for three years (see `01-competition/past_cases.md` Part III): prepared = arts/culture non-profit with a sustainability problem; boardroom = owner-led local consumer/family business at a growth or positioning crossroads; traditional = large Manitoba-rooted enterprise with a trust/talent/digital-engagement challenge — always with a Manitoba anchor. Scan Winnipeg's arts scene and prominent local firms before February.
