# 05 · Practice cases — what we train on

Simulation material for Phases 2–4, in order of value. Aim for variety across the three round formats and
the recurring ABCC client types. Ten simulations are scheduled in
[`../02-plan/week_by_week_plan.md`](../02-plan/week_by_week_plan.md); every result goes into
[`../02-plan/simulation_log.md`](../02-plan/simulation_log.md) the same day.

## 1. Real ABCC cases first — the best material there is

**Eleven official past cases (2023–2026) across ten clients** (2023 ran both rounds on Manitoba Hydro). Six
PDFs are mirrored in [`../01-competition/past-case-pdfs/`](../01-competition/past-case-pdfs/); the other five
(2024 cases 1 and 3, all three 2026 cases) download from the URLs in
[`../01-competition/facts_and_rules.md`](../01-competition/facts_and_rules.md). All eleven are profiled with
**re-crack briefs** — task, guiding questions, and what a winning answer needed — in
[`../01-competition/past_cases.md`](../01-competition/past_cases.md).

**Recommended order:** Manitoba Museum 2025 (prepared) → People Corporation 2025 (traditional) → AROW Global
2025 (boardroom) → Independent Jewellers 2024 (boardroom) → Access CU / Jazz Winnipeg / Coal & Canary 2026
from the re-crack briefs → the Manitoba Hydro 2023 pair for framing drills. **StandardAero 2024 (traditional)
is held back for the W25 competition-week simulation**, where it runs as a 10-hour case.

Seven of the ten planned simulations run on these.

past that marker, until they have presented.

## 5. Other sources, when this folder runs dry

- **Other competitions' released cases and finals videos** (John Molson MBA ICC, JMUCC) — winning-team videos
  are prime study material for delivery and Q&A.
- **Ivey / Harvard case collections** — short cases (5–15 pages) via the university library, for timed
  structuring drills.
- **Consulting-club casebooks** (free online) — market sizing and structuring.
- **Self-written mini-cases** — one-page prompts on real regional companies with 3–4 public exhibits; cheap
  and fresh.

## The 2027 pattern to train against

The client formula has been stable for three years (see
[`../01-competition/past_cases.md`](../01-competition/past_cases.md) Part III): **prepared** = arts/culture
non-profit with a sustainability problem · **boardroom** = owner-led local consumer or family business at a
growth or positioning crossroads · **traditional** = large Manitoba-rooted enterprise with a trust, talent or
digital-engagement challenge — always with a Manitoba anchor. Scan Winnipeg's arts scene and prominent local
firms before February.

**2027 twist:** the third round becomes a **10-hour case** (prep Fri Feb 19, presentations Sat Feb 20 —
assumed to replace the 3-hour traditional until the organizers confirm; see
[`../01-competition/facts_and_rules.md`](../01-competition/facts_and_rules.md)). Expect the same client type
but a judge bar set for ten hours: sourced outside research, fuller financials, a month-level
implementation plan. Both the W17 (Sparkasse) and W25 (StandardAero) simulations train that format.

> **Case data in the original cases is fictional** and labelled as such; the companies are invented.
> Teaching-note figures are calibration aids, not real-company facts.
